About Us

I am an IT professional specializing in technical support, computer networking, systems administration, web development, and ICT education. With experience in IT consulting and hands-on technical instruction, I combine practical troubleshooting skills with a passion for building reliable technology solutions and empowering others through digital skills. My technical experience spans Windows and Linux environments, computer hardware repair and maintenance, MikroTik network configuration, web technologies, database management, and server administration. I also explore virtualization, containerization, and private cloud technologies to build and manage efficient IT infrastructures. Through my work as an ICT Instructor at Enlight Vocational Institute and my previous experience in IT consulting at Gamers Arena Custom PC, I have developed a practical, solution-oriented approach to technology—supporting users, maintaining computer systems, configuring networks, developing websites, and delivering hands-on ICT training. My goal is to use technology to solve real-world problems, improve digital access, and help individuals and organizations work smarter, connect better, and grow confidently in an increasingly digital world.
