Most computer failures give a warning first. If you notice any of these signs, it is a good time to have the machine checked.

## 1. It has become very slow

A computer that takes minutes to start or freezes while you work may have a full drive, too little memory, unwanted programs running, or a failing hard disk.

## 2. The fan is loud all the time

Fans get loud when dust blocks the airflow and the machine cannot cool itself. Cleaning the dust often fixes it.

## 3. It gets very hot or shuts down by itself

Overheating can make a computer switch off to protect itself. Dust and old thermal paste on the processor are common causes.

## 4. Strange noises from the hard disk

Clicking or grinding from a traditional hard disk is a serious warning. **Copy your important files to another place right away**, before the disk stops working.

## 5. Programs crash or error messages keep appearing

This can come from software faults, a virus, or failing hardware. Finding the real cause is better than reinstalling blindly.

## What you can do today

- Back up your important files.
- Do not keep a laptop on a bed or a soft cushion, because it blocks the air vents.
- Book a check-up before the problem gets worse.
