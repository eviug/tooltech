Most online problems start with a small mistake, not clever hacking. These five habits stop a large share of them.

## 1. Use a different password for important accounts

If one website is breached, criminals try the same password everywhere else. Use a long password made of several words, and never reuse your email password.

## 2. Turn on two-step verification

Add a code sent to your phone for your email, mobile money and social media accounts. Even if someone learns your password, they cannot get in.

## 3. Be careful with links and messages

- Be suspicious of messages that create panic or promise free money.
- Do not share your PIN or one-time codes with anyone, even someone who says they are from a bank or a network company.
- Check the address of a website before you type a password.

## 4. Keep your software updated

Updates fix security holes. Install them on your phone, computer and browser.

## 5. Back up what matters

Keep a copy of important files on a flash disk or in the cloud, so a lost phone or a virus does not mean lost work.
