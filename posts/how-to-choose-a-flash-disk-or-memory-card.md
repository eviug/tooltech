A flash disk or memory card looks simple, but a poor one can lose your files at the worst moment. These are the points we check before recommending one.

## 1. Pick the capacity you really need

Photos, music and documents take much less space than videos. A small card is fine for a basic phone, while a phone that records a lot of video needs more room. Buy a little more than you think you need, because files always grow.

## 2. Buy from a seller you can trust

Fake cards and disks exist. They claim a large size but only hold a fraction of it, and files quietly disappear once the real space is full. Buy genuine brands from a shop that will answer questions after the sale.

## 3. Check what your device can read

- Older phones and cameras may only accept smaller memory cards.
- Newer phones with a Type C port can use a Type C flash disk directly.
- A phone with no USB port for storage can often use an **OTG adapter** to read a normal flash disk.

## 4. Look after it

- Always use "Eject" or "Safely remove" before pulling a disk out of a computer.
- Keep a second copy of anything important. A flash disk is a carrier, not a safe.
- Do not leave it in a hot place such as a car on a sunny day.
